How it works

Names Gbam teaches AssemblyAI

Speech recognition often mishears Nigerian names and market goods, like "Mom and Gozi" for Mama Ngozi. So every time you start talking, Gbam gives AssemblyAI the customer names and products in your record book below. This demo uses a sample shop, Mama Bisi Provisions. When you add a new customer, their name joins the list straight away. See the difference it makes.
